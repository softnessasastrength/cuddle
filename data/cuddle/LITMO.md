# cuddle 1.4.0 — lo-fi Litmo map

This package is a local, file-less cut of protocols from:

https://github.com/softnessasastrength/Litmo

Litmo is a personal emotional containment system, not a public product.
cuddle keeps the craft invariants that still make sense in a terminal:

- Soft Signal is free (q). No explanation. No penalty. Stopping is success.
- Nothing is stored. Nothing is sent. Completing a flow is not a score.
- Not therapy, not diagnosis, not consent to touch.
- Armor, not homework. Skip anything. Re-enter in any order.

## Deliberately not ported

Conflict-sim, need-scared, relationship constitution, field notes,
debrief lab, and anything that needs a partner, a network, a Watch,
or a file. Litmo’s own rule: prefer honest reduction over nuclear
expansion.

`--tui` is a curses cathedral. `--hub` still opens the bash menus.
`--guide` still opens the original 1.1.0 consent branches.
